## The name

The name is SoMA. Of the four candidates it is the most distinctive, it already names the project in every document, and in Nepal it carries meanings that suit the app. The comparison stays here for the record.

| Name | What it says | In its favour | Against it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Posts | Exactly what the app does | Instantly clear; the page and store listing need no explaining | A common word: hard to find in app stores, and almost impossible to register as a mark |
| Society | People together | Ties to cosys, collaborative systems | Very broad; in South Asia the English word usually means a registered body or a housing society, so it reads as an institution; a "Societies" app is already on the App Store |
| SoMA | Social media app, as a backronym | Distinctive and already in use. In Sanskrit and Nepali, Soma (सोम) is the Moon and the Vedic ritual drink, and Monday is Sombar, so it links to coplanetary.work's Moon-and-beyond story. The *Brave New World* nod turns the pacifying drug into an app that gives ownership back | In English-speaking markets Soma is also a prescription muscle relaxant's brand, a lingerie brand and a video game, and the US register holds many SOMA marks; the drug echo needs the backronym told alongside it |
| Peers | People of equal standing; peer to peer | Matches nep.work and the peer-to-peer DNA; friendly | Close to apps already in the same category: a social networking app called Peer, and the PEERS® social-skills app |

The store listing can read "SoMA: posts you own", so people searching for posts still find it. Before launch, run a trademark search in software and social networking classes in Nepal and in each country you plan to list in. This is not legal advice.

## How it works

The same shape as the Diabetes App page's Snap, Share, Act, so the two pages feel like one family.

> **How it works**
>
> 1. **Write once.** Write on your phone and add photos, sound, PDFs or video clips of up to two minutes. The post is signed on your phone before it goes anywhere, and drafts follow you to your other devices.
> 2. **Post everywhere.** SoMA shapes the post for each network. Nostr gets it straight from your phone, and Mastodon and Bluesky get it through bridges. For apps like Instagram, X or LinkedIn, your phone's share sheet opens them with the post ready to go.
> 3. **Keep the proof.** Every edit is a new signed version of the same post. The final version is timestamped with OpenTimestamps, which anchors it in Bitcoin within a few hours, with no wallet and no coins. Share the post back from another app and SoMA keeps the link to that copy, private unless you choose to publish it.

## Anonymous and safe

This section answers the first worry about an anonymous app before anyone has to ask it, and it is also what App Store and Google Play reviewers look for.

> **Anonymous by default, safe by design**
>
> Your account starts as a fresh key with a generated name. We never ask for a phone number, an email or your legal name. You can keep separate personas that nobody can link unless you do, and photos lose their location and camera details before they leave your phone.
>
> Anonymous doesn't mean anything goes. You join by invitation from an operator who vets new members, so your pseudonym answers to a community without being tied to your identity. Every post has report, block and mute, and reports go to the operator's moderators. Private messages reach only people who follow you back, or people in your circles. There is no random chat.

## Where your posts go

A small table works on the site too, since visitors compare networks side by side. One line of introduction covers readers who have never heard of Nostr or the Fediverse.

> **One post, every network**
>
> Nostr is an open network where your identity is a key you hold. The Fediverse is thousands of independent servers that talk to each other, Mastodon being the best known. Bluesky is an open network built on the AT Protocol. SoMA reaches all of them, and the apps you already use.

| Network | How your post gets there | Replies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nostr | Straight from your phone to your relays | Come back to SoMA |
| Mastodon and the Fediverse | Through a bridge your operator runs; videos through PeerTube | Come back to SoMA |
| Bluesky | Through a bridge your operator runs | Come back to SoMA |
| Instagram, X, LinkedIn and others | Your phone's share sheet opens the app with the post ready | Share the link back to SoMA; it stays private unless you publish it |

## Circles, files and your data

Short blocks, each with its own heading on the page, in this order.

> **Your circles**
>
> Share with a family, a club or a team as a circle. Circle posts are sealed so only members can read them, and they pass from phone to phone with no server in the middle. When someone leaves, the circle gets a new key, so they can't read what comes next.
>
> **Works with no signal**
>
> Write, read and edit offline. SoMA catches up as soon as you're connected, and shows when each post last synced.
>
> **Photos, sound, PDFs and video**
>
> Post photos, sound, PDFs, maps and data files, and video clips of up to two minutes in playlists. Only open, safe file types are allowed, and nothing plays until you tap it.
>
> **Your posts stay yours**
>
> Your posts live on your phone and your own relays first, and every network's copy links back to the signed original. Take everything with you whenever you like, or delete your account from inside the app. The app tells you plainly that copies other people kept may remain.
>
> **Plugins in plain sight**
>
> Extras, such as viewers for new file types, come built into the app and are listed in Settings with what each can read and write. Nothing is downloaded behind your back.
